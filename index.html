Mortado: WHO adolescent mortality analytics platform

An end-to-end data platform that takes WHO adolescent mortality indicators from raw files to dashboards, models and a natural-language assistant, with scheduled runs and automated tests on AWS.

  1. IngestWHO adolescent mortality data
  2. CleanStandardize indicators and codes
  3. TestAutomated checks catch data quality failures
  4. StoreCleaned data in AWS RDS
